Achievements — 2025

Artificial Intelligence

Our students in GCE 2025 demonstrated their creativity through remarkable practical projects. Most notably, they developed a smart system to classify customer reviews in restaurants, a project to analyze and categorize people’s emotions to make applications more human-centered, and a system to accurately classify traffic signals to support road safety. These projects illustrated how simple ideas can be transformed into innovative solutions using artificial intelligence.

Web Development

This event introduces our intensive program for web development students. Over just a few weeks, students progress from the basics of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to building modern interfaces with React, working in teams (Git/GitHub, Code Review), and turning real designs into fast, responsive websites. The result? Graduates ready for the job market, with strong portfolios, the ability to deploy and launch, and hands-on understanding of user interfaces and user experience. Quick examples of our students’ projects: • A reusable marketing page with a component system and light/dark mode, built with React and Tailwind. • A dashboard for a small store displaying sales and inventory with interactive charts (React + Chart.js) and instant filtering.

Graphic Design

In 2025, our students showcased their creativity through innovative graphic design projects that brought community-driven ideas to life, alongside the redesign of existing products. Their work featured the development of complete visual identities, including logo creation, eye-catching promotional materials, dynamic social media content, as well as mockups and posters. These projects highlighted an exceptional level of creativity and professionalism. “Nivea and RedBull”. was among the most notable projects.

Network

In 2025, our students successfully carried out a range of advanced networking projects, including building networks from scratch, applying diverse protocols, and configuring the routing systems. Conducted in a real laboratory setting (Real lab), these projects emphasized practical skills while bridging theory with real-world application. As a result, students gained invaluable hands-on experience in network design and operations.
